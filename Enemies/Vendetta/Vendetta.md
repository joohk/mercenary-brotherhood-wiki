Named rivals that grow out of ordinary enemies and hunt your company: [[Glossary#Vendetta|Vendetta]].

> When an ordinary enemy kills one of your mercenaries, survives a battle, or escapes, they may become a Vendetta — a named rival who remembers the encounter, grows stronger, adapts to how you fight, and ambushes your squad on the map. The Vendetta Chance dial sets how readily they form.

## How it works

- **Power level.** A vendetta has a power level that rises as it survives. New abilities unlock at power levels 3, 5 and 7.
- **Traits.** It collects traits: 8 *adaptations* that counter the way you beat it (ranged attacks, melee, flanking, magic…), 7 *power* traits and 3 *behavioural* traits.
- **Name.** It is named from its faction's pool of first names, plus titles and ranks (see below).
- **Trophy.** When you finally kill it, it drops a trophy item — see [[Vendetta Trophies]].
- The [[Veteran Band]] founding starts with a vendetta already on your trail: [[Bandits]], power level 3, titled *Old Rival*.
- The [[Arcane Conclave (Founding)|Arcane Conclave]] founding makes vendettas form more readily (×1.5 chance).

## Death speeches

When a vendetta dies it may speak a last line, chosen by its mood:

> [!quote]- Terrified
> - "Please... no more... I yield!"
> - "Mercy! I beg you, mercy!"
> - "I should have run when I had the chance..."
> - "You... you were always going to win, weren't you?"
> - "Don't... don't let them remember me like this..."

> [!quote]- Defiant
> - "You think this ends here? My followers will avenge me!"
> - "Curse you and your company! I'll haunt you from beyond!"
> - "Strike me down. A hundred more will rise in my place!"
> - "This changes nothing. NOTHING!"
> - "I'll see you in the dirt, *name*. Sooner than you think."

> [!quote]- Respectful
> - "Well fought. I could not have asked for a worthier end."
> - "You earned this victory. Remember my name."
> - "A good death. Better than I deserved."
> - "I see now why my warriors feared your company."
> - "Finish it. You've proven yourself."

> [!quote]- Vengeful
> - "Kill me and two more will rise. You cannot stop what's coming."
> - "My warband will carry on. This changes nothing for your company."
> - "Every one of my followers will hunt you for this. Every. One."
> - "You've made an enemy of my entire clan today."
> - "My lieutenants already have their orders. Killing me won't save you."

> [!quote]- Resigned
> - "So this is how it ends. At least it was a good fight."
> - "I knew this day would come. I just hoped it would be later."
> - "Get it over with. I'm tired of running."
> - "We were never so different, you and I."
> - "Tell your people... tell them I fought well."
