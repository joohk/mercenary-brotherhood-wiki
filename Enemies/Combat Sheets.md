## About these sheets

*Least-resisted physical* is the slash/pierce/blunt type with the lowest average resistance across the faction's units (natural + worn gear), a hint for what to bring. Resistances are percentages: positive values reduce that damage type, negative values are a weakness (more damage taken). Totals add the unit's natural resistance and its worn armor, helmet and shield; the game caps resistance at 90%. Every faction also has a regular page with lore, encounter tables and villains: see [[Factions]].
