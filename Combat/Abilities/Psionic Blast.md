A 1-AP magic ability targeting one enemy up to 3 hexes away.

> Unleash a wave of psionic energy at a nearby mind. To-hit is a willpower contest: 50% + half the gap between your willpower and the target's, -10% per tile beyond adjacent. Strong-willed minds and fellow Deepkin resist it; the undead are immune. Deals 15-25 armor-ignoring damage and reduces target willpower by 20 (refreshes, does not stack).
