A 1-AP support ability targeting an adjacent ally.

> Grab an adjacent small unit — friend or foe — and hurl it up to 4 tiles to an empty space, dealing 5-10 impact damage (reduced by armor). A grabbed ally is lobbed at the enemy's softest line; a grabbed foe is flung clear to break a swarm. Provokes no opportunity attacks.
