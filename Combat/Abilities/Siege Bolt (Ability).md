A 1-AP ranged ability targeting an area.

> Fire a heavy explosive bolt at a target or an open tile. The target takes the bolt in full and every other enemy within radius 1 takes 50% and is knocked back a tile. Structures take triple damage as a crushing blow, so it breaches walls and doors. Requires a crossbow.
