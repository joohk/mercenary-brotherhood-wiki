A combat skill from the Defense group, with 10 levels and 9 skill traits.

> Shoulder to shoulder. Shield to shield. Gains XP from fighting with 2+ allies adjacent. At L5 you become a formation anchor: adjacent allies gain +3 melee defense while you and they form a line of 2 or more.

## How to train

**Trained by:** Attack or get attacked with 2+ allies adjacent.

**Discovery:** Can be discovered on a *formation fight* event; discovery weight 0.7.

**Tutorial offer:** support list (#2). In the tutorial's guaranteed first level-up, units whose gear does not already suggest a skill are offered skills from these ordered lists.
