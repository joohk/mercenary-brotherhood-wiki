> Mechanical ranged weaponry demands patience and precision over raw speed. Improves accuracy and armour-piercing power of heavy bolts. From L5 every crossbow hit sunders its target: -2 Armor and -5% Physical Resist for the rest of the battle, stacking up to 4 times.

**Restriction:** Bonuses only apply when using crossbows.

**Discovery:** Can be discovered on a *ranged hit* event involving crossbow-tagged gear or spells; discovery weight 0.4.
