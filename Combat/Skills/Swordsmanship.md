A weapon skill from the Weapon group, with 10 levels and 8 skill traits.

![[_assets/Icons/tier_1/sword_1h.webp|64]]

> Refined blade technique honed through repetition. Improves both accuracy and cutting power, weighted toward clean, precise strikes. From L5, Measured Strikes: each consecutive sword hit on the same foe gives +5% to hit, +10% damage and ignores 5% of its resistances, stacking up to 3 times. A miss or a new target resets it. Every level adds sword damage.

## How to train

**Trained by:** Land a melee hit with a sword equipped.

**Restriction:** Bonuses only apply when using swords.

**Discovery:** Can be discovered on a *melee hit* event involving sword-tagged gear or spells; discovery weight 0.5.

Weapon and armor skills level up as you use the matching gear; the data gives no per-level use thresholds for them.
