A combat skill from the Magic group, with 10 levels and 7 skill traits.

> The orcish and goblin blood-tradition: heal through violence, not prayer. Its restorative power rides weapon strikes (it scales with your damage, not magic power), and its hexes turn a marked foe into a feast for the whole warband. Initiates know Spirit Leech. Only orcs and goblins carry the gift.

## How to train

**Trained by:** Use a Shamanism ability in combat.

**Discovery:** Can be discovered on a *spill blood* event; discovery weight 1.

**Only discoverable by:** [[Orc]], [[Goblin]].
