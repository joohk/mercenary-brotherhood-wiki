A combat skill from the Melee group, with 10 levels and 10 skill traits.

> A skirmisher's craft: strike from a foe's side arc and slip its answer. Each side-arc hit gives you Footwork and Sidestep, and each level adds to the last.

## How to train

**Trained by:** Land a melee hit in an enemy's side arc.

**Discovery:** Can be discovered on a *side arc hit* event; discovery weight 0.6.
