A combat skill from the Ranged group, with 10 levels and 10 skill traits.

> Ranged control. Every ranged weapon hit lays 1 Suppression (up to 5): -2 Melee and Ranged Attack a stack, for 2 of the foe's turns. From level 5, Pinned Down: reaching 5 stacks spends them and costs the foe 1 AP on its next turn. Level 10 teaches Kill Zone.

## How to train

**Trained by:** Land a ranged hit on a foe that moved this round or is Suppressed.

**Discovery:** Can be discovered on a *suppress hit* event; discovery weight 0.5.

**Tutorial offer:** ranged list (#4). In the tutorial's guaranteed first level-up, units whose gear does not already suggest a skill are offered skills from these ordered lists.

Weapon and armor skills level up as you use the matching gear; the data gives no per-level use thresholds for them.
