> You pick off the ones who break cover. Gains XP from ranged hits on enemies that moved this round.

## How to train

**Discovery:** Can be discovered on a *suppress hit* event; discovery weight 0.5.

**Tutorial offer:** ranged list (#4). In the tutorial's guaranteed first level-up, units whose gear does not already suggest a skill are offered skills from these ordered lists.
