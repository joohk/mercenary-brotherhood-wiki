A combat skill from the Ranged group, with 10 levels and 9 skill traits.

> Long-range precision and heavy shots. Ranged Attack, damage and crit chance with any ranged weapon. From level 5, Range Finder: +3% damage and +1 crit chance for each tile beyond 4, up to 5 tiles. Level 10 teaches Sniper's Nest.

## How to train

**Trained by:** Land a ranged hit at 4+ tiles distance.

**Discovery:** Can be discovered on a *long range hit* event; discovery weight 0.6.

**Tutorial offer:** ranged list (#2). In the tutorial's guaranteed first level-up, units whose gear does not already suggest a skill are offered skills from these ordered lists.

**Bonuses apply to:** ranged hits only.

Weapon and armor skills level up as you use the matching gear; the data gives no per-level use thresholds for them.
