> Blunt-force mastery. Armour means little against a practiced mace strike; each level improves armour penetration and concussive power. From L5, a melee hit that deals 12+ damage has a 15% chance to Stun.

## How to train

**Trained by:** Land a melee hit with a mace equipped.

**Restriction:** Bonuses only apply when using maces.

**Discovery:** Can be discovered on a *melee hit* event involving mace-tagged gear or spells; discovery weight 0.5.

Weapon and armor skills level up as you use the matching gear; the data gives no per-level use thresholds for them.
