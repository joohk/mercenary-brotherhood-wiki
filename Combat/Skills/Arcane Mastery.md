A combat skill from the Magic group, with 10 levels and 12 skill traits.

> Deep study of arcane forces expands your reserves and sharpens your spells. Each level increases Max Fatigue and spell potency. Initiates know Magic Missile. At L10 a Spell Echo follows each spell — your next spell costs 0 AP and half fatigue.

## How to train

**Trained by:** Cast an arcane spell in combat.

**Discovery:** Can be discovered on a *spell cast* event involving arcane, fire, lightning and ice-tagged gear or spells; discovery weight 1.
