A playable race ([[Glossary#Numbers used on this wiki|recruitment threshold]] 12), weakest in Willpower and Max HP compared with other races.

> Small, wiry rat-folk with twitchy whiskers and keen senses. Born into teeming warrens, they act fast and hit above their weight when swarming. The civilized eastern ratkin forge disciplined armies of honor-bound soldiers; the western swarms scuttle through ruins plaguing whatever they touch. Both share the same keen nose and nimble claws.
