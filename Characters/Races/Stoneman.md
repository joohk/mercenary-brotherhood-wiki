A playable race ([[Glossary#Numbers used on this wiki|recruitment threshold]] 15), strongest in Armor and Max HP and weakest in Initiative and Ranged Attack compared with other races.

![[_assets/Units/StonemanSwordsman.webp]]

> Not all stone is dead. Stonefolk are ancient beings of living rock, implacable and nearly unkillable. They can no more bleed than a cliff face can. They move with the urgency of a glacier and couldn't hit a broad side of a barn with a bow, but once they reach an enemy, the fight tends to end quickly and messily.
