> Half the size and twice the luck. Halflings dodge blows that would floor a grown warrior, act before almost anyone else, and vanish into a crowd. Their supernatural luck grants them a chance to shrug off critical hits and a natural knack for dodging. They can't hit hard, but they're remarkably hard to kill outright.
