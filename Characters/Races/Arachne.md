A non-recruitable race ([[Glossary#Numbers used on this wiki|recruitment threshold]] 15), strongest in Ranged Attack and Ranged Defense and weakest in Melee Attack and Max HP compared with other races.

> [!warning] Not recruitable
> Retired from the hire pools: Arachne still appear in settlements, guard rosters and warbands, and any already on a roster keep working, but they can no longer be recruited.

![[_assets/Units/ArachneSword.webp]]

> Eight eyes, perfect aim, and venom that never misses the point. Arachne are terrifying ranged fighters who web and poison at will. Up close they are skittish and brittle — they prefer to watch you die from a safe distance with several arrows in your throat.
