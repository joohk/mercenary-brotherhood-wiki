A playable race ([[Glossary#Numbers used on this wiki|recruitment threshold]] 15), strongest in Initiative and Melee Defense and weakest in Willpower and Max HP compared with other races.

![[_assets/Units/WraithkinSwordsman.webp]]

> Part shadow, part warrior. Wraithkin slip through defences like smoke, strike from angles that should be impossible, and terrify enemies with a gaze that speaks of the void. Incoming abilities — even friendly ones — can phase through them harmlessly. Their willpower is their weakness; they feed on fear, and starve without it.
