A Battlemage specialization. Tracks *Fire* (every 3) **and** *Frost Kills* (every 3) — both must climb; 2 points per threshold.

> Fire, frost, and lightning braided into one storm. Kill with fire and frost to master it — and your lightning strikes harder too.
