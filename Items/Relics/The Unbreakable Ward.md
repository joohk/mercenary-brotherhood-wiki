## Awakening

The relic awakens at each kill threshold. Stages are cumulative: every stage reached keeps its bonuses and its curse.

### Stage 1 — Steadfast (30 kills)

> Behind the Ward, fear cannot reach you. Your resolve hardens like the shield itself.

- **Bonus:** +5 [[Stats#Willpower|Willpower]]
- **Curse:** -3 [[Stats#Initiative|Initiative]]

### Stage 2 — Bulwark (90 kills)

> The Ward has weathered a hundred blows in your hands. Nothing gets past it now.

- **Bonus:** +3 [[Stats#Armor|Armor]]
- **Bonus:** +5 [[Stats#Melee Defense|Melee Defense]]
- **Curse:** -3 [[Stats#Melee Attack|Melee Attack]]

### Stage 3 — The Immovable (180 kills)

> You have become the wall. Unbreakable, unyielding — and unhurried.

**Perk:** Capstone: grants Hold the Line — a sustained stance that shields nearby allies. If you already know it, its fatigue cost is reduced instead.

- **Bonus:** +6 [[Stats#Melee Defense|Melee Defense]]
- **Bonus:** +6 [[Stats#Ranged Defense|Ranged Defense]]
- **Bonus:** +8 [[Stats#Willpower|Willpower]]
- **Bonus:** Grants ability: [[Hold the Line (Ability)|Hold the Line]]
- **Bonus (if already known):** [[Hold the Line (Ability)|Hold the Line]]: -4 fatigue cost
- **Curse:** -5 [[Stats#Initiative|Initiative]]
