## Awakening

The relic awakens at each kill threshold. Stages are cumulative: every stage reached keeps its bonuses and its curse.

### Stage 1 — Half-Lidded (30 kills)

> The Eye blinks. For a heartbeat you see arrows before they are loosed.

- **Bonus:** +4 [[Stats#Ranged Defense|Ranged Defense]]
- **Curse:** -3 [[Stats#Initiative|Initiative]]

### Stage 2 — Open Eye (90 kills)

> The Pattern unfolds before you. Your aim finds gaps no living eye could see — but the waking world dims.

- **Bonus:** +5 [[Stats#Ranged Attack|Ranged Attack]]
- **Bonus:** +2 [[Stats#Vision|Vision]]
- **Curse:** -5 [[Stats#Melee Defense|Melee Defense]]

### Stage 3 — Unblinking (180 kills)

> You see what the seer saw, and it is too much. The Eye never closes now, and neither do yours.

**Perk:** Capstone: grants Flurry — a rapid multi-shot that strikes before foes can react. If you already know it, its cooldown is reduced instead.

- **Bonus:** +5 [[Stats#Ranged Attack|Ranged Attack]]
- **Bonus:** +6 [[Stats#Ranged Defense|Ranged Defense]]
- **Bonus:** Grants ability: [[Flurry (Ability)|Flurry]]
- **Bonus (if already known):** [[Flurry (Ability)|Flurry]]: -1 turn cooldown
- **Curse:** -5 [[Stats#Max HP|Max HP]]
- **Curse:** -4 [[Stats#Initiative|Initiative]]
