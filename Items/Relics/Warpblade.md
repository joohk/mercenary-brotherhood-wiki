## Awakening

The relic awakens at each kill threshold. Stages are cumulative: every stage reached keeps its bonuses and its curse.

### Stage 1 — Flickering (30 kills)

> You flicker too now. For an instant you exist in two places, and the enemy strikes the wrong one.

- **Bonus:** +4 [[Stats#Melee Defense|Melee Defense]]
- **Curse:** -2 [[Stats#Max HP|Max HP]]

### Stage 2 — Phasing (90 kills)

> The two blades move as one in your hand, faster than thought. The boundary between worlds thins around you.

- **Bonus:** +4 [[Stats#Melee Attack|Melee Attack]]
- **Bonus:** +4 [[Stats#Initiative|Initiative]]
- **Curse:** -3 [[Stats#Max HP|Max HP]]

### Stage 3 — Between Worlds (180 kills)

> You cut in this world and the next at once, as the Weaver did. But existing in two places frays the body that belongs to one.

**Perk:** Capstone: grants Phase Strike — blink adjacent to a foe and strike before they can react. If you already know it, you gain an extra use per battle instead.

- **Bonus:** +5 [[Stats#Armor Pen|Armor Pen]]
- **Bonus:** +4 [[Stats#Melee Defense|Melee Defense]]
- **Bonus:** Grants ability: [[Phase Strike]]
- **Bonus (if already known):** [[Phase Strike]]: +1 use per battle
- **Curse:** -5 [[Stats#Max HP|Max HP]]
