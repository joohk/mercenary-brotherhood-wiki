## Awakening

The relic awakens at each kill threshold. Stages are cumulative: every stage reached keeps its bonuses and its curse.

### Stage 1 — Recognized (30 kills)

> The inscription has settled. It reads your name now. The blade has accepted you.

- **Bonus:** +5 [[Stats#Willpower|Willpower]]
- **Curse:** -3 [[Stats#Ranged Defense|Ranged Defense]]

### Stage 2 — Sworn (90 kills)

> You have kept faith with the blade, and it answers in kind — but it brooks no caution, only conviction.

- **Bonus:** +4 [[Stats#Melee Attack|Melee Attack]]
- **Bonus:** +3 [[Stats#Damage Min|Damage Min]]
- **Curse:** -3 [[Stats#Melee Defense|Melee Defense]]

### Stage 3 — The Oath Kept (180 kills)

> The one oath Ash kept is yours now. The blade is unbreakable in your hand — and so is your refusal to yield.

**Perk:** Capstone: grants Battle Cry — steel the resolve of all nearby allies. If you already know it, you gain an extra use per battle instead.

- **Bonus:** +4 [[Stats#Damage Min|Damage Min]]
- **Bonus:** +5 [[Stats#Damage Max|Damage Max]]
- **Bonus:** +5 [[Stats#Willpower|Willpower]]
- **Bonus:** Grants ability: [[Battle Cry (Ability)|Battle Cry]]
- **Bonus (if already known):** [[Battle Cry (Ability)|Battle Cry]]: +1 use per battle
- **Curse:** -5 [[Stats#Ranged Defense|Ranged Defense]]
