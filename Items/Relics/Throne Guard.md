## Awakening

The relic awakens at each kill threshold. Stages are cumulative: every stage reached keeps its bonuses and its curse.

### Stage 1 — Mending (30 kills)

> The broken crown on the shield has mended itself. Your guard has never felt more sure.

- **Bonus:** +3 [[Stats#Armor|Armor]]
- **Curse:** -3 [[Stats#Initiative|Initiative]]

### Stage 2 — Crowned (90 kills)

> The mended crown gleams. Those behind you stand firmer — and you will not be moved.

- **Bonus:** +5 [[Stats#Melee Defense|Melee Defense]]
- **Bonus:** +5 [[Stats#Willpower|Willpower]]
- **Curse:** -3 [[Stats#Melee Attack|Melee Attack]]

### Stage 3 — The Throne (180 kills)

> You are the still point the battle breaks against, as the bearer was. An immovable throne — and just as slow to rise.

**Perk:** Capstone: grants Shield Formation — project a defensive formation over nearby allies. If you already know it, its fatigue cost is reduced instead.

- **Bonus:** +3 [[Stats#Armor|Armor]]
- **Bonus:** +5 [[Stats#Ranged Defense|Ranged Defense]]
- **Bonus:** Grants ability: [[Shield Formation]]
- **Bonus (if already known):** [[Shield Formation]]: -4 fatigue cost
- **Curse:** -5 [[Stats#Initiative|Initiative]]
