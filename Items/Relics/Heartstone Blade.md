## Awakening

The relic awakens at each kill threshold. Stages are cumulative: every stage reached keeps its bonuses and its curse.

### Stage 1 — First Taste (30 kills)

> The blade's heartbeat quickens. It has tasted blood, and it wants more.

- **Bonus:** +2 [[Stats#Damage Min|Damage Min]]
- **Bonus:** +2 [[Stats#Damage Max|Damage Max]]
- **Curse:** -2 [[Stats#Max HP|Max HP]]

### Stage 2 — Bloodbound (90 kills)

> It drinks from the slain, not just the wielder. The crystal runs red beneath the surface.

**Perk:** Lifedrink — heal 2 HP on each melee hit.

- **Bonus:** +4 [[Stats#Melee Attack|Melee Attack]]
- **Bonus:** +5 [[Stats#Armor Pen|Armor Pen]]
- **Bonus:** On melee hit: Heal 2 HP
- **Curse:** -3 [[Stats#Max HP|Max HP]]
- **Curse:** -3 [[Stats#Willpower|Willpower]]

### Stage 3 — The Heart Devours (180 kills)

> The heartbeat is your own now. Every kill feeds the blade, and the blade feeds on you.

**Perk:** Bloodfeast — heal 8 HP on each kill; also grants the Blood Feast ability (an armor-ignoring life-drain strike). If you already know it, you gain an extra use per battle instead.

- **Bonus:** +4 [[Stats#Damage Min|Damage Min]]
- **Bonus:** +6 [[Stats#Damage Max|Damage Max]]
- **Bonus:** On melee kill: Heal 8 HP
- **Bonus:** Grants ability: [[Blood Feast]]
- **Bonus (if already known):** [[Blood Feast]]: +1 use per battle
- **Curse:** -5 [[Stats#Max HP|Max HP]]
- **Curse:** -3 [[Stats#Initiative|Initiative]]
