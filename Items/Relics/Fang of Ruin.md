## Awakening

The relic awakens at each kill threshold. Stages are cumulative: every stage reached keeps its bonuses and its curse.

### Stage 1 — Stirring (30 kills)

> The faces on the axe are screaming louder now. Each kill adds a new voice to the chorus.

- **Bonus:** +4 [[Stats#Damage Max|Damage Max]]
- **Curse:** -3 [[Stats#Melee Defense|Melee Defense]]

### Stage 2 — Howling (90 kills)

> The chorus is deafening. The axe drags you forward into the slaughter, heedless of danger.

**Perk:** Rending — your attacks leave foes Bleeding.

- **Bonus:** +5 [[Stats#Melee Attack|Melee Attack]]
- **Bonus:** +4 [[Stats#Damage Min|Damage Min]]
- **Bonus:** On hit: Inflicts Bleeding I (5 bleed damage per tick for 3 ticks)
- **Curse:** -5 [[Stats#Ranged Defense|Ranged Defense]]

### Stage 3 — Ruin Unbound (180 kills)

> The nameless thing's hunger is yours. You exist only to end things, and you are very good at it.

**Perk:** Devour — heal 6 HP on each kill; also grants Cleave (a sweeping strike against all adjacent foes). If you already know it, its cooldown is reduced instead.

- **Bonus:** +5 [[Stats#Damage Min|Damage Min]]
- **Bonus:** +7 [[Stats#Damage Max|Damage Max]]
- **Bonus:** On melee kill: Heal 6 HP
- **Bonus:** Grants ability: [[Cleave (Ability)|Cleave]]
- **Bonus (if already known):** [[Cleave (Ability)|Cleave]]: -1 turn cooldown
- **Curse:** -6 [[Stats#Melee Defense|Melee Defense]]
- **Curse:** -4 [[Stats#Max HP|Max HP]]
