## Awakening

The relic awakens at each kill threshold. Stages are cumulative: every stage reached keeps its bonuses and its curse.

### Stage 1 — Clinging (30 kills)

> The shadows cling to you now, even in daylight. You move before others can react.

- **Bonus:** +5 [[Stats#Initiative|Initiative]]
- **Curse:** -3 [[Stats#Max HP|Max HP]]

### Stage 2 — Half-Seen (90 kills)

> Blades pass through where you seemed to be. The mantle has learned your shape and hides it.

- **Bonus:** +6 [[Stats#Melee Defense|Melee Defense]]
- **Bonus:** +6 [[Stats#Ranged Defense|Ranged Defense]]
- **Curse:** -2 [[Stats#Armor|Armor]]

### Stage 3 — Umbral (180 kills)

> You are more shadow than flesh now. Nothing can catch you — but there is so little of you left to catch.

**Perk:** Capstone: grants Shadow Walk — slip through the shadows to a distant hex. If you already know it, its cooldown is reduced instead.

- **Bonus:** +6 [[Stats#Initiative|Initiative]]
- **Bonus:** +5 [[Stats#Melee Defense|Melee Defense]]
- **Bonus:** Grants ability: [[Shadow Walk]]
- **Bonus (if already known):** [[Shadow Walk]]: -1 turn cooldown
- **Curse:** -6 [[Stats#Max HP|Max HP]]
