## Awakening

The relic awakens at each kill threshold. Stages are cumulative: every stage reached keeps its bonuses and its curse.

### Stage 1 — Warming (30 kills)

> The red veins pulse brighter after each battle. The cuirass is feeding.

- **Bonus:** +8 [[Stats#Max HP|Max HP]]
- **Curse:** -3 [[Stats#Initiative|Initiative]]

### Stage 2 — Engorged (90 kills)

> The plate has thickened, drinking the blood of the slain. It grows heavier with power.

**Perk:** Bloodforge — regenerate 3 HP at the start of each of your turns.

- **Bonus:** +4 [[Stats#Armor|Armor]]
- **Bonus:** At turn start: Heal 3 HP
- **Curse:** -3 [[Stats#Melee Attack|Melee Attack]]

### Stage 3 — Ironblood Heart (180 kills)

> The cuirass beats with a heart of its own now, gorged on a hundred battles. It will not let you fall — nor let you run.

**Perk:** Bloodforge deepens — regenerate 6 HP/turn total; also grants Second Wind (a large on-demand self-heal). If you already know it, you gain an extra use per battle instead.

- **Bonus:** +13 [[Stats#Max HP|Max HP]]
- **Bonus:** +4 [[Stats#Armor|Armor]]
- **Bonus:** At turn start: Heal 3 HP
- **Bonus:** Grants ability: [[Second Wind (Ability)|Second Wind]]
- **Bonus (if already known):** [[Second Wind (Ability)|Second Wind]]: +1 use per battle
- **Curse:** -6 [[Stats#Initiative|Initiative]]
