Game keywords and mechanics, as defined by the in-game Guide and tooltips.

## Arch-Villain

**One of eight fixed named bosses who awaken and threaten the world as you grow.**

Eight arch-villains are placed in the world at the start of every game. They awaken as your company grows, move daily, corrupt nearby settlements, and ambush your squad if you wander too close. Defeating one removes them permanently and drops a unique legendary relic.

*Also:* arch-villains, villain, villains

*See also:* [[Glossary#Vendetta|Vendetta]], [[Glossary#Relic|Relic]]

## Armor

**Flat damage subtracted from every hit before HP is lost.**

Armor subtracts a flat amount from every hit before it reaches HP, capped so it can never absorb more than about 70% of a single blow. Armor Penetration on the attacker reduces the target's effective armor before this reduction is applied.

*See also:* [[Glossary#Armor Penetration|Armor Penetration]]

## Armor Penetration

**Ignores this much of a target's armor before its damage reduction applies.**

Armor Penetration ignores a flat amount of the target's armor before its damage reduction is calculated. Daggers and certain traits add penetration; some awakening effects ignore armor entirely.

*Also:* pen

*See also:* [[Glossary#Armor|Armor]]

## Ascendancy

**The pinnacle of growth; a demanding capstone with one of four paths.**

Ascendancy is the peak of a unit's growth. Eligibility is demanding: high level and potential tier, an epithet, a specialization, at least 100 kills, and proven war record. The ceremony offers four of eight demigod paths: War, Protection, the Hunt, Endurance, Pain, Death, Magic, and Life. Only two living units may be ascended at once; a slot reopens if a demigod dies or leaves the company.

*Also:* ascended, ascend

*See also:* [[Glossary#Awakening|Awakening]], [[Glossary#Specialization|Specialization]]

## Awakening

**Crossing a Potential tier lets a unit choose a powerful tier-unique awakening trait.**

When a unit crosses a Potential tier threshold it can undergo an Awakening, choosing a powerful trait unique to that tier and locking in the tier's benefits. Each level-up after awakening consumes 150 potential, so units that level quickly may drop tiers.

*Also:* awakened, awaken

*See also:* [[Glossary#Potential|Potential]], [[Glossary#Ascendancy|Ascendancy]]

## Cover

**Terrain between shooter and target lowers ranged hit chance and absorbs damage.**

Forest, ruins, and obstacles on the line between a shooter and its target reduce ranged hit chance and absorb a fraction of the damage that lands. Firing at a foe locked in melee with an ally is harder still. Reposition for a clean line of sight.

*See also:* [[Glossary#Flanking|Flanking]]

## Critical Hit

**A chance for an attack to deal bonus damage; rear attacks add crit chance.**

Each attack has a chance to critically hit for bonus damage — base 5% chance for +50% damage. Gear, traits, and specializations raise both. Striking a target from the rear adds +5% crit chance.

*Also:* critical, crits

*See also:* [[Glossary#Flanking|Flanking]]

## Dodge

**A chance to avoid an attack that would otherwise hit. Capped at 40%.**

Dodge is a chance to avoid an attack that would otherwise land, on top of your defense. It is capped at 40%. Some effects (Wind Dancer, Evasive Roll, Phase Shift) instead grant a chance to completely negate an incoming attack.

*See also:* [[Glossary#Initiative|Initiative]]

## Fatigue

**Builds as units act; the more tired a unit is, the worse it fights.**

Every action costs fatigue. As units tire they move through Fresh, Winded, Tired, Fatigued, and Exhausted stages, losing initiative first and then attack, defense, and dodge. Units shed a little fatigue each turn and far more by resting (End Turn without attacking). High Willpower speeds recovery.

*Also:* fatigued, exhausted, exhaustion, tired, winded

*See also:* [[Glossary#Willpower|Willpower]], [[Glossary#Initiative|Initiative]]

## Flanking

**Attacking outside a foe's front arc grants bonus hit chance (and crit from the rear).**

Every unit faces a direction. Striking from the side (flank) grants +10% to hit; striking from behind (rear) grants +20% to hit and +5% crit. Ranged attacks get half the bonus. A unit turns to face its target after attacking, opening its back to others.

*Also:* flank, flanked, facing, rear

*See also:* [[Glossary#Cover|Cover]], [[Glossary#Critical Hit|Critical Hit]]

## Hidden Depths

**Secret traits some recruits carry that reveal themselves as the unit fights.**

Some recruits harbor Hidden Depths — secret traits that reveal themselves as the unit fights, explores, or reaches milestones. Watch for hints during recruitment; hidden depths can unlock unique abilities as the unit gains specializations.

*See also:* [[Glossary#Trait|Trait]], [[Glossary#Specialization|Specialization]]

## Initiative

**Determines turn order; higher acts sooner and cuts the speed cost between turns.**

Initiative sets where a unit falls in the turn order. Higher initiative acts sooner and reduces the speed cost between turns. It is the first stat fatigue erodes, so tired units act less often. There is a soft cap above which extra points give less.

*See also:* [[Glossary#Fatigue|Fatigue]]

## Magic Power

**Bonus to spell damage and support-spell healing. Does not affect spell accuracy.**

Magic Power increases offensive spell damage and support-spell healing once a spell connects — it does not change whether the spell lands. It is the Magic Power stat plus Willpower above 40. Ranged Attack is a caster's accuracy with magic.

*See also:* [[Glossary#Willpower|Willpower]], [[Stats#Ranged Attack|Ranged Attack]]

## Officer Role

**Appointed members grant company-wide benefits and level up through their deeds.**

As your company level rises you can appoint members to officer roles — Captain, Quartermaster, Champion, Scout Master, and more — each granting company-wide benefits. Roles level to 5 through their own deeds. You may field one active role per five living members.

*Also:* officer, captain, quartermaster

*See also:* [[Glossary#Reputation|Reputation]]

## Potential

**A hidden growth resource earned in combat that raises trait rarity and XP gains.**

Potential accumulates from combat: 100% of a foe's value for a kill, 25% for an assist, 5% for being present. It is organised into 10 tiers; higher tiers make rare and legendary traits appear more often at level-up and add +5% XP per tier, up to +25%. Fight tougher enemies to build it faster.

*See also:* [[Glossary#Awakening|Awakening]], [[Glossary#Trait|Trait]]

## Relic

**A powerful unique item won from an arch-villain, with a mandatory drawback.**

There are 12 unique relics, each won from an arch-villain. Relics are extremely powerful but carry a mandatory curse. They also hide further effects that unlock as the carrier scores kills, so a relic grows into its full power in the right hands.

*Also:* relics

*See also:* [[Glossary#Arch-Villain|Arch-Villain]]

## Reputation

**Each settlement and realm tracks its standing with your company.**

Beyond a settlement's opinion of you, the realms themselves track your standing. Quests, cleared bases, and battles within a realm's borders shift how it — and its allies — regard you. Strong standing opens better work and warmer welcomes; a poor one closes doors.

*See also:* [[Glossary#Siege|Siege]]

## Siege

**A faction marches war parties on a settlement; ride in during the assault to defend.**

When a faction's aggression boils over it raises war parties and marches on a settlement. A siege builds for several days before the assault; ride there during the assault to fight in its defense. Some are led by a Warlord whose defeat breaks the campaign. Leave a siege unanswered and the settlement is sacked.

*Also:* sieges, invasion, warlord

*See also:* [[Glossary#Warband|Warband]], [[Glossary#Reputation|Reputation]]

## Skill

**Learned by doing; each level unlocks a skill-specific trait choice.**

Skills are learned through use — swing a sword enough and Melee Mastery emerges. Each skill has multiple levels, and leveling one unlocks a choice of skill traits that enhance it. New skills also surface through skill discovery when you fight in new ways.

*Also:* skills

*See also:* [[Glossary#Trait|Trait]], [[Glossary#Specialization|Specialization]]

## Specialization

**Every 4 levels a unit may pick a specialization that tracks a combat metric.**

Every 4 levels a unit may choose a Specialization based on its combat experience. Each tracks a metric (kills with a weapon type, damage absorbed, flanking kills) and has 3 levels of growing bonuses. Weapon-mastery specs apply only while the matching weapon is equipped.

*Also:* specializations, spec

*See also:* [[Glossary#Skill|Skill]], [[Glossary#Trait|Trait]]

## Trait

**A permanent passive bonus chosen at level-up; rarity scales with Potential.**

Traits are permanent passive bonuses — extra damage, crit, armor, and more. The rarity of the three traits offered at level-up depends on the unit's Potential tier. Some race + background pairings unlock exclusive combo traits.

*Also:* traits

*See also:* [[Glossary#Potential|Potential]], [[Glossary#Skill|Skill]]

## Vendetta

**A named enemy promoted from an ordinary foe who then hunts your company.**

When an ordinary enemy kills one of your mercenaries, survives a battle, or escapes, they may become a Vendetta — a named rival who remembers the encounter, grows stronger, adapts to how you fight, and ambushes your squad on the map. The Vendetta Chance dial sets how readily they form.

*Also:* vendettas, nemesis, rival

*See also:* [[Glossary#Arch-Villain|Arch-Villain]], [[Glossary#Warband|Warband]]

## Warband

**An ordinary roaming enemy group — a wandering threat, not a recurring character.**

Warbands are the ordinary enemy groups that roam the overland map. They are wandering threats, not persistent characters like Vendettas or arch-villains. Some patrol territory, some clash with rival forces in field battles you can join.

*Also:* warbands

*See also:* [[Glossary#Vendetta|Vendetta]], [[Glossary#Siege|Siege]]

## Willpower

**Resistance to morale checks; above 40 it also aids fatigue recovery and magic.**

Willpower governs how well a unit holds its nerve under pressure. Above 40 it boosts fatigue recovery, Magic Power, and Second Wind healing. Your mercenaries never flee, but Shaken units fight worse. Enemies whose willpower breaks may flee the field.

*Also:* morale, shaken, resolve

*See also:* [[Glossary#Fatigue|Fatigue]], [[Glossary#Magic Power|Magic Power]]

## Numbers used on this wiki

These are not in-game glossary entries. They explain raw numbers from the game data that pages show.
Meanings marked *inferred* come from how the data is used, not from a stated definition.

- **Company level** — your mercenary company's level. Most content is gated by it: enemies, encounter groups, quests and
  settlements list a `company level` range in which they appear.
- **Tier** — power band. Enemies run 0–5 (0 = desperate fodder, 5 = the strongest units); items run 0–7
  (0 = basic enemy-only gear, 1–6 = shop and loot tiers, 7 = relics).
- **Spawn weight** — relative chance to be picked when the game rolls among eligible candidates (enemies of a faction,
  items in a shop or loot roll). Only meaningful against other weights in the same pool. **0 = never picked at random**
  (only appears when something names it explicitly).
- **Weight** (encounter groups, magic properties, lair kinds) — same idea: relative odds among the eligible options.
- **Threat cost** — *inferred:* how much of an encounter's budget an enemy uses up; stronger enemies cost more, so a
  fight has fewer of them.
- **XP / Gold value** — experience and gold the company earns for defeating that enemy.
- **Potential value** — present on enemies but undocumented in the data.
- **Hire cost ×** (backgrounds) — multiplier on a recruit's hiring price: ×0.6 is cheap, ×2.5 very expensive.
- **Recruitment threshold** (races) — undocumented in the data; *inferred:* how established your company must be before
  that race shows up for hire (0 = always available).
- **Trait odds** (backgrounds, combos) — small numbers added to the race's weights for rolling a common/uncommon/rare/
  legendary trait on level-up (`+0.15 uncommon` makes uncommon traits more likely).
- **Durations** — 1 turn = 100 time units in the data; pages convert to turns.
- **Percentages** — fractions in the data (0.25) are shown as percent (25%). "+12% Damage" multiplies damage by 1.12.
