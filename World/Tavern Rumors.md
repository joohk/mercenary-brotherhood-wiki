What you hear when you buy a round of drinks at a tavern.

## How it works

Buy a round of drinks at a [[Crossroads Tavern]] to hear rumors. Each round gives a few rumors, each picked from a category by weight and filled into one of that category's lines.

|  | Value |
|---|---|
| Drinks cost | 5 gold |
| Rumors per round | 1-2 |
| Most rumors per visit | 3 |

## Categories

| Category | What it tells you | [[Glossary#Numbers used on this wiki\|Weight]] | Chance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Point of interest | points you toward a nearby point of interest | 35 | 28.23% |
| Enemy intel | warns of a warband nearby | 30 | 24.19% |
| Quest lead | hints at the direction of your current contract's target | 20 | 16.13% |
| Treasure tip | tips you off to loot lying somewhere nearby | 15 | 12.1% |
| Ascended legend | retells the legend of a mercenary who ascended | 12 | 9.68% |
| War monument | recalls a famous deed of a mercenary company | 12 | 9.68% |
