A 3-stage quest chain from company level 3, with a 4% chance to trigger.

> A hostile faction targets your company with repeated ambushes.

## Stages

Stages are posted one after another; the delay is counted from the previous stage.

| Stage | Quest | Posted | Objective | Gold |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [[Ambush Survival First Strike\|Ambush Survival: First Strike]] | Immediately | Survive the *faction* ambush (5) | 110-200 |
| 2 | [[Ambush Survival Second Wave\|Ambush Survival: Second Wave]] | +5 days | Survive the *faction* ambush (7) | 170-280 |
| 3 | [[Ambush Survival Final Assault\|Ambush Survival: Final Assault]] | +5 days | Survive the *faction* final assault (9) | 210-350 |
