A 2-stage quest chain from company level 2, with a 6% chance to trigger.

> Track down and eliminate a persistent rival.

## Stages

Stages are posted one after another; the delay is counted from the previous stage.

| Stage | Quest | Posted | Objective | Gold |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [[Hunt Vendetta Gather Intel\|Hunt Vendetta: Gather Intel]] | Immediately | Defeat *faction* to find the vendetta (5) | 80-170 |
| 2 | [[Hunt Vendetta Confront the Rival\|Hunt Vendetta: Confront the Rival]] | +3 days | Destroy the vendetta's lair | 210-390 |

## Completion rewards

Paid on top of each stage's own rewards when the whole chain is done.

|  | Value |
|---|---|
| Bonus gold | 500 |
| Reputation | +10 |
| Capstone item | One rare item, tier 2-3 |
